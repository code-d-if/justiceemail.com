Why This Matters

Nearly two million people are incarcerated in the United States. For many of them, staying in touch with family, friends, and loved ones is difficult, expensive, and sometimes nearly impossible.

That isolation doesn't just affect the person inside — it touches everyone who cares about them. Children grow up without hearing from a parent. Partners struggle to maintain relationships across walls and distance. The bonds that help people rebuild their lives after incarceration quietly erode.

What FireFly Does

FireFly makes it easier and more affordable for incarcerated individuals to send and receive messages. By removing barriers to communication, FireFly helps families stay connected when it matters most.

The email you received was sent through FireFly by someone who wanted to reach out to you. That simple act of connection can make a real difference.
